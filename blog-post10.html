Starting my Master’s in Computer Science: Sep 2026 – Jan 2027

Nov 2026  ·  vlog  ·  life update

In September 2026 I started my Master’s in Computer Science. This first term, from September 2026 to January 2027, is the part of the journey I am living in right now, so this post is a status update more than a retrospective.

Why a Master’s

My undergraduate degree in Computer Science with an AI specialization gave me breadth. Teaching assistant work, research-style projects like the OpenVLA study and the superpixel GNN experiments, and full-stack products like LeetCloned and ChatiFly showed me what I enjoy most: understanding why a system behaves the way it does, then making it reliable.

I wanted time to go deeper on that, with structured research, stronger theory, and people who push back on my assumptions.

What the first term feels like

The biggest change is the expectation of independence. Fewer instructions, more open-ended problems, and more reading of real papers. As a teaching assistant I used to explain concepts to others. Now I am the one who has to defend my own reasoning.

Time management is also harder. Coursework, research interest, and the job search all compete for the same hours, so I plan my weeks around a short list of priorities instead of a long to-do list.

What I want out of it

I want one or two projects that go deep rather than many that stay shallow, stronger research habits, and a clear direction in AI systems and robotics-oriented machine learning. I also want to keep writing here, because explaining something is still the best test of whether I understand it.

Finishing a degree changes your title. What you build next changes your skills.

I will post updates as the term progresses.
